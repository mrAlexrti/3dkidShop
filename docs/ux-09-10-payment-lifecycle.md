# UX-09 account checkout payment lifecycle

This follow-up starts from review commit `75b0ec25d9d960c0fe2ceb44971c1e8e641b4361`. The previous implementation blocked authenticated checkout while *any* unresolved account payment existed. Closing the hosted page kept the cart and provisional attempt, but a changed cart or switch to cash on delivery could wait indefinitely. The operator approved automatic reconciliation of superseded unpaid attempts.

## Mono contract

- `POST /api/merchant/invoice/create` creates a debit invoice and returns an invoice ID and hosted URL. Invoice validity is in seconds, defaults to 24 hours at Mono, and is already set to 3,600 seconds by the storefront.
- `GET /api/merchant/invoice/status` checks the invoice using the merchant credential. The storefront compares invoice ID, checkout reference, amount and UAH currency. It applies the validated provider timestamp and financial state through the signed-webhook transition logic. Mono documents that callbacks can arrive out of sequence and ordinary unpaid expiry does not generate an `expired` callback. A local validity estimate governs whether to offer an old link. It cannot establish expiry.
- `POST /api/merchant/invoice/remove` deactivates an unpaid invoice. Its documented empty HTTP 200 confirms deactivation. A 404, timeout, 429, or provider error gives insufficient evidence to publish an overlapping replacement. The refund endpoint `invoice/cancel` remains separate. This checkout flow uses only the unpaid removal API.
- The webhook continues to check Mono's X-Sign signature. Before attaching a previously unknown invoice ID, it also checks that invoice with the merchant credential. A browser return cannot establish a financial result. [Mono acquiring API](https://api.monobank.ua/docs/acquiring.html) · [Mono invoice creation and webhook details](https://monobank.ua/api-docs/acquiring/dodatkova-funktsionalnist/tokenizatsiia/post--api--merchant--invoice--create)

## Checkout and recovery

| Evidence or action | Outcome |
| --- | --- |
| Tab closed | Keep the cart, provider reference and provisional attempt. |
| Same account, same contact/delivery, same current price and same purchase units | Reuse a validated hosted URL if merchant status is still `created` and its local display validity has not elapsed. A different signed-in device can recover it by submitting the same checkout details. |
| Modified checkout or a switch to COD | Match unresolved attempts by authenticated owner and overlapping units of the same cart line generation. Validate the new quote, reconcile the old invoice and remove it if confirmed unpaid and eligible. Only confirmed deactivation or authoritative terminal evidence releases those units for the replacement. The new order transaction rechecks price, availability and cart revision and records the predecessor link. |
| Failed charge or unusable hosted link | A failed charge alone is insufficient proof that its hosted page can no longer take a payment. On checkout, try to deactivate a failed invoice or an unpaid invoice with an unusable link; otherwise wait for authoritative expiry or resolution. An explicit status check leaves the invoice untouched. |
| Independent orders | Unrelated purchase units and previously accepted orders do not participate. A removed and deliberately recreated line has a new generation. Unpaid attempts created before account carts have no purchase plan and cannot safely be matched automatically. |
| Success wins before replacement | Accept the paid order once, allocate its order number, snapshot the existing notifications, and consume its remaining reserved quantity intervals in the same transaction. Later additions survive. Require review of the updated cart before another overlapping order. |
| Success arrives after supersession | Preserve the paid financial evidence and show `SUPERSEDED_PAYMENT_PAID` in administrator payment review. No second fulfillment, cart deduction, or automatic refund. Older or duplicate events cannot re-consume the cart. |
| Processing, held, 429, timeout or uncertain removal | Preserve the cart and give a localized retry/status path. Back off for 10 seconds or 30 seconds for a merchant 429. Only the overlapping purchase waits for evidence. A lost removal response followed by a 404 alone can require operator reconciliation. |
| Unknown create response, no saved ID or URL | Keep the attempt. A confirmed local create error without a persisted handoff, or 45 seconds after a crashed initialization with no ID or URL, permits supersession. The old handoff is fenced by the same customer lock and predecessor link, and authenticated late financial evidence still enters review. This is a handoff rule, not evidence that the provider invoice expired. |

Each stored purchase unit belongs to a line generation, with its interval derived from removed units and quantity. The existing customer row lock serializes order creation, acceptance and hosted handoff. Merchant calls run outside database transactions. Per-attempt leases prevent concurrent status/removal requests across browsers and survive a process crash. The final locked reservation rechecks related attempts, so a provider call returning after another checkout cannot publish a competing overlapping invoice. Same-request retries keep an HMAC-derived identity and verified terminal retries derive a stable successor. A past request cannot remove an unrelated successor. Each submit checks at most three unresolved overlapping attempts before the final recheck.

The authenticated payment result page also supports explicit **Check status** and, while the invoice is eligible, **Continue payment** after a fresh merchant check. The signed attempt reference and live owner session are both required to retrieve the hosted URL. The account history remains limited to accepted orders. A customer who lost the original tab can resubmit checkout, or use a still-valid signed result link while signed in. Guest checkout uses its existing flow.

## Reconciliation and rollout

There are no new storefront navigation requests or background provider polling. Payment status reconciliation runs during an overlapping checkout or an explicit owner status check. The existing authorized `/api/cron/payments` handler can reconcile known IDs in the background if an existing schedule calls it. A scheduler is useful for abandoned payments when customers never return and callbacks are missing. Confirm whether it is already configured during rollout. If absent and unattended detection is needed, seek separate approval to schedule the existing handler, with monitoring for errors and overdue rows and an agreed call cadence. Routine checkout and expiry recovery do not require a new schedule.

The complete [reviewed migration](release/ux-09-10-customer-cart.sql) supersedes the previous release SQL. It adds two fields to the new purchase-plan table, an indexed successor-order foreign key with delete restriction, and a check against self-links. The restricted runtime role can update just the two new purchase columns. An idempotent single-column Order grant allows the existing hosted handoff to write `paymentPageUrl` and is retained on rollback. The existing schema otherwise gains only the three planned cart tables and the owner trigger. No existing records are changed. Follow the [preflight, sentinel, verification and recovery sequence](ux-09-10-pre-release-review.md#database-release-and-recovery).

## Local verification

- The isolated PostgreSQL rehearsal uses the real order creation, attachment and acceptance transactions under `app_3dkid`. It checks unchanged existing-row fingerprints, the SQL sentinel, restricted grants, no schema drift, empty rollback and reapplication, and rollback refusal after cart use. The original fixture omitted the existing hosted URL update privilege. The migration now grants that column and the rehearsal exercises the actual handoff write.
- Synthetic Mono cases cover valid invoice reuse, failed charges, expired invoices without webhooks, online-to-COD switching, timeout and throttling, failed removal, success racing removal, duplicate and stale evidence, late paid review, independent orders, and create responses lost with or without an invoice ID. PostgreSQL assertions verify order identity, notifications and once-only cart deductions.
- The local optimized Edge regression covers authorized payment continuation and new checkout on desktop EN and mobile UA, with account and guest isolation. The broader suite checks browser hydration, UX-06 through UX-10, commerce, SEO and mocked OAuth. The checkout acceptance, cart UI and branded email checks use isolated fixtures.

### Live payment verification before launch

1. With separate payment authorization and a controlled account on two devices, start Mono checkout, close the tab and submit the same order again. Confirm both devices resolve to the same payable invoice.
2. Change the basket, submit again and verify in the merchant panel that the old unpaid invoice is deactivated before the newer link is offered. Repeat with a switch to COD. Reload the old provider URL to verify its actual behavior.
3. Let a controlled unpaid invoice expire without a callback. Confirm another checkout observes merchant expiry, retains the cart and can proceed. Confirm a genuinely failed payment can be retried safely.
4. Complete one authorized payment while changing the cart on another device. Verify the old order is accepted once, repeats do not deduct again and newer or unrelated cart items survive. Verify signed webhook delivery and the admin review path for any disputed paid evidence.
5. Verify copy and recovery on desktop and a physical phone in UA and EN, plus guest checkout. Confirm whether the existing scheduled reconciliation is enabled and check its status and backlog separately.
